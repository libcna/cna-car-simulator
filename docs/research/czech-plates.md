# Czech registration plates: research notes

Purpose: give the plate generator (`simulator/src/Traffic/PlateGenerator.cpp`) and the plate
texture renderer factual rules. Sources were reached through web search summaries and
GitHub-hosted references because the development environment's network policy blocks direct
access to Wikipedia, the Czech e-Sbírka portal and plate-collector sites; each fact below names
the source that supported it. Re-verify against the primary source (vyhláška č. 343/2014 Sb.,
o registraci vozidel, příloha č. 16) whenever plates are changed.

## Legal basis

- Vyhláška č. 343/2014 Sb., o registraci vozidel (amended, e.g. by 72/2019 Sb.), defines the
  tables (plates), their dimensions and the shapes and dimensions of characters (příloha 16).
  Source: search results for the decree text on dauc.cz / sagit.cz / e-sbirka.gov.cz.

## Current standard series (since 29 June -- 17 July 2001)

- Seven characters for cars, displayed as `1A2 3456`: digit, **regional letter**, digit or
  letter, space, four digits. Series start at `1x0 0001` where `x` is the regional letter.
  Source: Wikipedia summary ("Vehicle registration plates of the Czech Republic"), Škoda
  Storyboard "Deciphering number plates".
- Regional letters (kraj): A Praha, B Jihomoravský, C Jihočeský, E Pardubický,
  H Královéhradecký, J Vysočina, K Karlovarský, L Liberecký, M Olomoucký, P Plzeňský,
  S Středočeský, T Moravskoslezský, U Ústecký, Z Zlínský. (Search results confirmed A, B, C, S
  explicitly; the full list matches the project author's knowledge of the 2001 system and
  must be treated as "confirmed for A/B/C/S, consistent for the rest".)
- Letters **G, O, Q, W** and the digraph **CH** are never used (confusable with C, V and 0).
  Source: Wikipedia summary; validator.js `cs-CZ` pattern `[ABCDEFHIJKLMNPRSTUVXYZ0-9]`.
- When a region exhausts `9x9 9999`, the third character becomes a letter: `1xA 0000` ...
  `9xZ 9999`, alphabetical. Prague (A) reached this in 2009; South Moravian (B) and
  Moravian-Silesian (T) from summer 2014; Ústí (U) from April 2019. Source: Wikipedia summary.
- Dimensions: single-row plate **520 x 110 mm**; black characters on white, retro-reflective;
  characters **75 mm high, 40 mm wide, stroke about 11.5 mm**; typeface based on **DIN 1451**.
  Left blue band with twelve golden stars and the white letters `CZ`. Source: Wikipedia summary
  (font/character sizes), Škoda Storyboard (size, EU band).
- Two-row plates (e.g. 340 x 200 mm) exist for some vehicles; the simulator only uses the
  single-row car plate.

## Other series relevant to normal passenger traffic

- **Electric vehicles** (since 1 April 2019): `EL` followed by five characters, configuration
  `EL0 00AA` (letters and digits mixed). Source: Škoda Storyboard e-mobility article, decree
  wording via search ("Registrační značka elektrického vozidla je složena z velkých písmen EL
  a z dalších ... kterých je u automobilů 5"). Rare in traffic; optional in the generator.
- **Personalised plates** (registrační značka na přání, since January 2016): eight characters
  for cars, at least one digit, no G/CH/O/Q/W, no regional code. Source: decree wording via
  search. Not generated by default (rare and free-form).
- Historic (V), sport (R), diplomatic and temporary/export series are out of scope.

## Generator rules adopted

1. Standard plates only by default: `D R X DDDD` where `D` is a digit 1-9 in position 1 and
   0-9 elsewhere, `R` a regional letter drawn from a population-weighted table, `X` a digit, or
   for regions A, B, T, U a letter from `ABCDEFHIJKLMNPRSTUVXYZ` with a region-specific
   probability reflecting the share of two-letter series in circulation.
2. Never produce G, O, Q, W; never produce the digraph `CH` (cannot occur in the format anyway).
3. Optional `EL` plates with a small configurable share.
4. Plates are unique within a session; the generator is seeded so traffic is reproducible.
5. Rendering: 520:110 aspect, white face, black border, blue EU band with 12 stars and `CZ`,
   characters in a DIN-1451-like typeface (D-DIN, SIL OFL 1.1) scaled to 75/110 of the height
   and 40 mm advance, one space group after the third character.

## Not done / caveats

- Exact glyph shapes of the Czech plate typeface are not reproduced; D-DIN is an approximation.
- Region weights are estimates (population share), not registration statistics.
